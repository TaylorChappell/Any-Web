ANY Desktop 0.4.0

- Hold Enter on a poster, right-click it or use the three-dot icon for Resume, My List, Watched, thumbs up/down and Together.
- A compact details panel adds cast portraits and filmographies, regional age certification, release dates, TMDB vote counts and series status. Finish-time estimates use remaining runtime.
- Franchise progress, Resume next, dislike-to-skip, optional-entry labels and curated story order for the Skywalker saga and Indiana Jones. Other collections retain release order.
- Guest viewing leaves saved history, recommendations and ratings untouched.
- Retry or switch a stalled source while retaining the last confirmed position and episode. Delayed loading messages lead to recovery controls.
- CC menu selects the subtitle tracks a native source exposes, or turns them off. Some providers require their own subtitle controls.
- Together keeps the current episode and timeline during brief reconnects, then waits for fresh playback readiness.
- Tonight has up to 18 picks, stable selection chips and fewer repeats from the homepage. Home shelves remove repeated and unreleased titles; Continue watching uses portrait cards without trailers.

Update in Settings, or run the installer. Windows x64; unsigned installer.
Browser video controls still depend on the provider's supported API.
